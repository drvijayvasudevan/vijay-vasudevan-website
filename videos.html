You’ve heard that autistic people die at 36. That number is real, it was peer-reviewed, and it was never a life expectancy. The Format

Four Questions, Every Episode

A consistent lens for reading research critically
  1. 01

    What did the study find?

    The main results, stated plainly and without jargon.

  2. 02

    Do the methods hold up?

    Design, data, and analysis — and how much confidence the findings deserve.

  3. 03

    What does it mean in practice?

    Implications for care, services, programs, and policy.

  4. 04

    Who counts?

    Who was included in the research, who was left out, and why that matters.
